China's October crude oil imports rise to highest level since March

Reuters · 12 Nov 2026 · 812 words

ask about this

China's crude oil imports rose in October to the highest daily rate since March, customs data showed on Wednesday, as refiners restocked ahead of new import quotas and took advantage of softer prices.

Arrivals totalled 48.7 million tonnes last month, equal to about 11.5 million barrels per day, up 6.4% from September. Independent refiners in Shandong accounted for most of the increase, according to shipping analysts.

Imports for the first ten months were 1.8% lower than a year earlier, reflecting weaker fuel demand as electric vehicles take a larger share of new car sales.
